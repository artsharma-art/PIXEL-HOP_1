How to play

🎮

Controls

← → or A D to move. Space or ↑ to jump. Hold jump for a higher hop. On phones use the on-screen buttons.

🏁

Goal

Reach the red flag at the end of each level. Collect coins for points and finish with lives to spare.

👾

Enemies & spikes

Land on top of purple enemies to stomp them. Touching them from the side, or touching spikes and pits, costs a life.

💎

Power-ups

Blue stars give you one extra jump in mid-air. Moving platforms carry you across big gaps.

About

Pixel Hop is a small, free platformer built to run right in your browser. Pick Easy for more lives and slower enemies, Normal for the intended challenge, or Hard for 2 lives and faster everything. Your best score for each difficulty is saved on this device, and to your account when you are logged in. New to platformers? Run the tutorial first — it takes about a minute.

Account

Sign up to save your scores and appear on the leaderboard. You can still play without an account.
